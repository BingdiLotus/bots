This is the single-Bot MVP trial release, not a production-HA release.

## Highlights

- One Bot / one Slack workspace / one model: persistent knowledge, native reminders, cross-session recall, thread-scoped commands and deterministic Slack sending.
- Chronotope integration for finite runs, tool bridge, durable Restate timers and recovery after Bots/worker restart.
- Slack least privilege: `app_mentions:read`, `channels:history`, `chat:write`, `groups:history`; Socket Mode and signed webhook share one admission path.
- Operator surface: admin API/page, readiness probe, `unknown` delivery resolution with audit, pause/disable, knowledge deactivation and delivery/task/audit views.
- Trial operations: independent trial tenant/channel, loopback-only `/metrics`, `scripts/trial-health.sh`, backup/restore and full-stack recovery evidence.
- D1 PG PITR, D2 RustFS replay and D3 Restate restore passed; same-window Bots+Chronotope+Restate restore measured service RTO 18.3s and RPO 0 in the local dev stack.

## Known limits

- MVP scope remains one Bot; multi-Bot/peer is test-only and is not a product capability in this release.
- The real-Slack fault matrix is partial: several de-duplication, unknown/late-receipt, reschedule/cancel and isolation rows are covered by G0/PG/fake, not injected into real Slack.
- G2 covers one SOP case plus four guards; two checker false positives were preserved, not hidden.
- The trial is a local Docker deployment; systemd/Linux target-host validation, production HA and continuous PITR are not included.
- Trial owner/opt-in, alert thresholds, notification channel and rollback ownership still require confirmation before expanding a real team trial.
- AI multi-Bot experiments are synthetic and do not replace real human trust/usability validation.

## Upgrade and rollback

- Run the image by digest above with the existing seed/env separation; credentials remain local and gitignored.
- Restore Bots schema or the full-stack volumes using the documented drills; do not use `down -v` as a recovery substitute.
- Roll back by reverting to the previous image digest and keeping the same PG/Restate data; the tag does not migrate data or historical threads.
- Rebuild from the tagged commit rather than a rolling `latest` tag.
